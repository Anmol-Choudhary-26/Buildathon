## Product Hunt-style description

BLR Home Hunt is a privacy-first flatmate matching platform for Bengaluru. It combines neighbourhood-level map discovery, commute-distance search, lifestyle profiles, transparent costs, and an approval-based contact-sharing flow.

## Ask users these questions

1. Could you find a relevant listing within two minutes?
2. Was the map location private enough while still useful?
3. Which decision detail was missing: photos, rules, budget, move-in date, commute, or flatmate preferences?
4. Did the request-and-approval process feel clear and safe?
5. What would make you trust a host or seeker more?
6. Would you invite a friend to use it? Why or why not?

## Highest-value improvements

1. **Listing verification:** Verify phone number, identity, and optionally ownership or tenant status.
2. **Report and moderation tools:** Add report, block, suspicious-listing review, and admin moderation before growing acquisition.
3. **Better matching:** Add deal-breakers and preferences such as smoking, dietary preferences, pets, sleep schedule, and language.
4. **Saved searches and alerts:** Notify seekers when a matching listing appears near their chosen office or landmark.
5. **Media management:** Add reorder, delete, cover-image selection, upload progress, and video thumbnails.
6. **Real route times:** Replace the road-distance estimate with a routing provider and show peak/off-peak commute time.
7. **Trust signals:** Show profile completeness, response rate, last active time, and verified badges without exposing PII.

## Metrics to track during the first launch

- Sign-up to completed profile rate
- Listing publish success rate
- Search-to-request conversion
- Host response rate and median response time
- Accepted-request rate
- Report/block rate
- Seven-day returning users

## Safety promise to publish

> We do not show exact property addresses publicly. Names, emails, and phone numbers stay encrypted and are only shared between a host and seeker after an accepted request.
